# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Windows and macOS users who prepare maimai charts or otherwise need to correct an audio file's starting offset. They typically work with one song at a time and need a fast, dependable desktop workflow without learning a full digital audio workstation.

## Product Purpose

XMaoMusic OffsetEditor imports a local audio or NCM file, removes time from its beginning or prepends silence, converts between common audio formats, detects BPM, applies explicit output parameters, and exports a compatible file. Success means the user can inspect the source, make a precise offset correction or format conversion, understand the resulting parameters and duration, and finish without manual environment setup or a separate NCM application.

## Brand Personality

Precise, fluid, restrained. The product should feel focused and technically trustworthy while retaining a sense of motion appropriate to sound and timing.

## Anti-references

- A browser landing page with oversized promotional copy or feature marketing.
- A neon cyberpunk or gaming overlay filled with saturated purple, glowing outlines, and decorative HUD elements.
- A stack of translucent cards where blur reduces hierarchy or readability.
- A generic utility form with no visual connection to waveforms, timing, or audio flow.

## Design Principles

- Put the single-file correction workflow ahead of decoration.
- Make every parameter's effect and applicability understandable before export.
- Use motion to communicate audio state, selected operation, and progress.
- Preserve familiar desktop affordances while removing setup friction.
- Follow the operating-system appearance on first launch and persist an explicit light/dark choice.
- Use native macOS window chrome and dependable system-level dragging on custom Windows chrome.
- Fail clearly, keep source files untouched, and never overwrite output silently.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Respect the operating system's reduced-motion preference and provide an explicit background-motion toggle. All core controls must have visible focus states, keyboard operation, readable Chinese labels, and non-color-only status cues.
